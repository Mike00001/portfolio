# Why the ESP32?

Unlike traditional Wi-Fi dongles requiring a Kali Linux host machine (like Alfa Network chips), the ESP32 offers the advantage of absolute compactness and low power consumption. By modifying the SDK to allow promiscuous mode, the ESP32 can listen to all raw 802.11 frames passing through the air. This project leverages this feature for passive reconnaissance (wardriving) and active operations.

# Key Features Implemented

  • Passive Analysis (Sniffing): Network scanning (Hidden SSIDs, WEP, WPA), EAPOL handshake capture saved in PCAP format on SD card.
  • Deauthentication Attacks: Targeted transmission of deauth frames to force a specific client's disconnection in order to capture the WPA handshake upon reconnection.
  • Beacon Spamming: Ability to flood the environment with fake networks (thousands of SSIDs generated per second) to test the resilience of surrounding Wi-Fi clients.

# Precautions and Ethics

Warning: This device was designed for educational and internal audit purposes with explicit authorization. Intercepting wireless communications or disrupting networks (such as denial of service / deauth attacks) on unowned infrastructures is illegal.

During testing, the tool is configured to only interact with filtered BSSIDs (those of my own test labs), ensuring complete isolation to avoid affecting neighborhood networks.

# Skills Acquired

Writing low-level C++ firmware, in-depth understanding of IEEE 802.11 standards (MAC Header, Frame Control, Management Frames), and rigorous memory management in constrained embedded systems (FIFO queue management for packet capture to SD).
